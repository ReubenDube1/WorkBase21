About WorkBase21

Built to connect South African talent with real opportunity.

WorkBase21 is a job portal built for South African job seekers and employers. We bring together jobs, internships, learnerships, in-service trainee positions and bursaries in one clean, fast, and easy-to-use platform.

Whether you're a graduate looking for your first internship, a student searching for a bursary, or an experienced professional seeking your next role, WorkBase21 is designed to make the search simple and the process transparent.

Our Mission

To reduce the friction between talented people and the organisations that need them — with a platform that is fast, trustworthy, and genuinely useful for both sides of the market.

For Employers

WorkBase21 gives you a clean, professional way to reach motivated candidates across every stage of their career, from students seeking bursaries to experienced professionals ready for their next challenge.
