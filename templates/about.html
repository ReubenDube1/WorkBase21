About WorkBase21

Built to connect South African talent with real opportunity.

WorkBase21 started with a simple frustration: finding a job, internship, learnership or bursary in South Africa means checking a dozen different websites, company career pages, and social media groups — and still missing opportunities that were never on your radar.

After spending months searching for internships and bursaries myself and losing track of deadlines across different sites, I decided to build the platform I wished existed — one place where every opportunity is easy to find, verified, and up to date.

Today, WorkBase21 brings together jobs, internships, learnerships and bursaries from across South Africa in one clean, fast, and easy-to-use platform — built for job seekers, not recruiters.

Our Mission

To reduce the friction between talented people and the organisations that need them, with a platform that is fast, trustworthy, and genuinely useful for both sides of the market.

We believe that:

  • Finding an opportunity shouldn't require checking ten different websites.
  • Listings should be accurate, current, and easy to understand — no vague deadlines or missing details.
  • Job seekers deserve free access to career guidance, not just a database of listings.

Who We're For

Students and graduates looking for their first internship, learnership, or bursary to fund their studies.

Early-career professionals searching for their next role or a structured programme to build experience.

Experienced professionals looking for their next opportunity without wading through irrelevant listings.

How We Work

Every listing on WorkBase21 is reviewed before it's published. We link directly to the employer's or issuing organisation's own application process — we never ask you to pay to apply, and we never require you to submit sensitive personal information through our site. If you're ever unsure whether a listing is genuine, our guide to verifying opportunities can help.

Alongside listings, our Career Resources section publishes original, practical guidance — CV writing, interview preparation, and honest takes on the South African job market — written for the realities job seekers actually face here.

For Employers

WorkBase21 gives you a clean, professional way to reach motivated candidates across every stage of their career — from students seeking bursaries to experienced professionals ready for their next challenge.

If you'd like to list an opportunity with us, please get in touch via our Contact page.

Who's Behind WorkBase21
