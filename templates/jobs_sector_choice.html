Public sector or private sector? How applying differs

The two kinds of employer hire in quite different ways, so it helps to know what to expect before you start preparing an application.

Public sector jobs

These are posts in government departments, provincial and local government (municipalities) and state-owned entities. Adverts normally state a closing date, the required qualifications and experience, a reference number and a contact person. Most ask for a completed Z83 application form and a CV. Some adverts ask you to attach certified copies of your ID and qualifications straight away; others ask only shortlisted candidates for them later. Read each advert carefully and follow its exact instructions, because incomplete or late applications are usually not considered. Selection can take weeks or even months.

Private sector jobs

Companies usually ask for a CV and a short cover letter, often through an online portal or by email. The process is often quicker, with one or more interviews, and the salary may be negotiable. Tailoring your CV to the specific role matters more here, because there is often no fixed form and no set selection criteria to follow.

Whichever you choose

Check the closing date and the requirements before you apply, apply only through the channel given in the advert, and never pay anyone to apply or to “secure” a job. Our guides in the Blog cover how to avoid fake listings and how to prepare your documents.
